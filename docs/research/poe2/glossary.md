# PoE2 Glossary

Fast-lookup reference for Path of Exile 2 terminology and abbreviations. One line per term. Where a PoE1 (original Path of Exile) concept does **not** carry over to PoE2, or works differently, that's called out explicitly — don't assume PoE1 knowledge transfers.

## Last updated
September 16, 2026

---

## A

- **Affix** — A modifier (prefix or suffix) rolled onto a Magic or Rare item.
- **Ailment** — A status effect applied by damage type: Ignite, Chill, Freeze, Shock, Bleed, Poison.
- **ARPG** — Action Role-Playing Game; the genre PoE2 belongs to (real-time combat + deep itemization/build systems).
- **Ascendancy** — A subclass chosen mid-campaign per base class, unlocking a second, class-specific passive-tree web via Ascendancy Trials.
- **Ascendancy Trial** — A gauntlet instance required to earn Ascendancy points. PoE2 has two: Trial of the Sekhemas and Trial of Chaos.
- **Atlas** — The endgame world-map screen where Waystones are launched and endgame progression/currency is tracked.
- **Atlas (Passive) Tree** — A separate passive tree spent on endgame/Atlas-mechanic points; distinct from the character's main passive tree.
- **Attribute** — Strength / Dexterity / Intelligence; core stats gating gear and skill-gem requirements.
- **Aura Stacking** — A build strategy that runs many simultaneous Auras (self-cast and/or minion-granted) to multiply their bonus effects; enabled by high Spirit capacity and reservation-efficiency gear.

## B

- **BiS (Best in Slot)** — The theoretically optimal item for a given gear slot in a specific build.
- **Boss Rush / Pinnacle Boss** — Top-difficulty optional endgame bosses; typically the highest loot and challenge tier available.
- **Build Enabler** — An item (often Unique) a build cannot function without; removing it breaks the build's core mechanic entirely, not just weakens it.

## C

- **Charm** — A PoE2 equipment slot separate from Flasks, mainly used for ailment removal/immunity utility effects.
- **Chaos Orb** — A commonly-traded mid-tier currency item; frequently used as the game's informal baseline pricing unit.
- **Checkpoint** — A PoE2 in-zone revive/fast-travel point, more granular than a Waypoint (new relative to PoE1).
- **Corrupted** — An item permanently altered (and locked out of further crafting) by a corruption currency (Vaal Orb); can add a powerful or a ruinous random effect.
- **Crit (Critical Strike)** — A hit that deals bonus damage at a percentage chance, scaled by Crit Chance and Crit Multiplier stats.
- **Currency Exchange** — PoE2's built-in, automatic order-matching market for stackable currency items; lists what you have/want and the system matches offers — no whisper negotiation needed.

## D

- **Delirium** — An encounter/league mechanic that fogs an area, buffing monster difficulty and reward density.
- **Delve / Delving** — A PoE1 mining-cart endgame system. **Not** a core PoE2 mechanic as of the current early-access build; don't assume it's present unless a specific patch/league adds it.
- **Div Card (Divination Card)** — A PoE1 stackable item that trades in for a fixed reward when a full set is collected. **PoE2 does not currently have Divination Cards.**
- **DoT (Damage over Time)** — Damage applied across several ticks rather than instantly (ignite, poison, bleed, etc.).

## E

- **EHP (Effective Health Pool)** — Total damage a character can absorb before dying, combining Life, Energy Shield, armor, evasion, and resistances.
- **ES (Energy Shield)** — A secondary "shield" health pool that depletes before Life and regenerates faster than Life; a core defensive layer many builds stack instead of (or alongside) Life.
- **Essence** — A currency type that crafts a Magic or Rare item with one guaranteed affix.
- **Exalted Orb** — A higher-tier currency item, historically used to add value to already-good gear; also a common bulk-trading unit.

## G

- **GGG (Grinding Gear Games)** — The New Zealand-based studio that develops and publishes Path of Exile and Path of Exile 2.
- **Gold** — A PoE2-specific universal currency, separate from orbs, used for gear repairs, respec costs, and vendor purchases (new relative to PoE1).

## H

- **Hardcore (HC)** — A game mode where character death is permanent; the character is moved out of the Hardcore league (into Standard/its softcore equivalent) on death.
- **HCSSF** — Hardcore + Solo Self-Found combined: permadeath and no trading.
- **Hideout** — A player's private, customizable instanced base area.

## I

- **IIQ / IIR** — Increased Item Quantity / Increased Item Rarity; stats that boost how much loot drops and how good it is.
- **Instant Buyout** — A trade-site/in-game filter that lets a buyer purchase directly from a seller's Merchant Tab listing with no whisper/response wait.

## J–K

- **Juicing** — Deliberately stacking modifiers or currency investment into a map/encounter to maximize its loot density or value.
- **Keystone** — A major passive-tree node that fundamentally changes a game rule (e.g., trading one resource or mechanic for another).

## L

- **League** — A timed seasonal reset (commonly ~3 months) with a fresh economy and a new headline mechanic; characters merge into Standard when the league ends.
- **Linking / Link** — In PoE1, gear sockets had to be physically "linked" and color-matched for a Support Gem to affect a Skill Gem (a "six-link" being the max). **PoE2 removed color/link requirements** — supports slot directly onto a skill without off-color problems, so "six-link" in the old sense is not current PoE2 terminology.

## M

- **Magic Find (MF)** — A build/gear archetype stacking IIQ/IIR to farm valuable drops, typically trading away survivability or clear speed to do it.
- **Mark** — A PoE2 skill type functioning similarly to a single-target curse/debuff.
- **Merchant Tab** — A premium stash-tab type that lists its contents for sale on the trade site and in-game Market panel, enabling Instant Buyout.
- **Meta** — The currently dominant, community-agreed-strongest builds or strategies for the active patch/league.
- **Mirror of Kalandra** — The rarest currency item in the game; duplicates any item exactly, including its corruption state. Extremely high value.

## N–O

- **Notable** — A mid-tier named passive-tree node granting a meaningful stat cluster; weaker than a Keystone, stronger than a small/minor node.
- **Omen** — A currency-modifier item that changes the effect of the next currency item used (e.g., redirecting or guaranteeing an outcome).

## P

- **Passive Tree** — The large web of skill points every character allocates into for stats and build-defining nodes.
- **Pen (Penetration)** — A damage stat that ignores a portion of an enemy's resistance to that damage type.
- **poe2db** — A community-run database site mirroring official PoE2 game data (items, gems, mods, monsters) for reference/lookup.
- **poe2scout** — A community site aggregating live PoE2 trade-site listings into currency/item price data — an economy tracker in the spirit of PoE1's poe.ninja. (Project Vaal's own `/prices` page sources live icon/price data from this site's public feed.)
- **Portal** — A scroll or skill that opens a temporary link back to town/hideout from anywhere in a zone.

## R

- **Rare** — A yellow-named item bearing multiple random affixes; below Unique rarity, above Magic.
- **Regal Orb** — Currency that upgrades a Magic item to Rare, adding an additional affix in the process.
- **Respec** — Reallocating already-spent passive or Atlas tree points, at a cost (limited, and/or currency-gated).
- **RMT (Real-Money Trading)** — Buying or selling in-game currency, items, or accounts for real money; against GGG's Terms of Service.
- **Rune** — An item socketed into a piece of gear's dedicated rune slot for a small flat stat bonus; separate from skill/support gem sockets.

## S

- **Season** — Colloquial synonym for a League cycle.
- **Six-Link** — See **Linking**; a PoE1 term for a fully-linked 6-socket item. Not applicable to PoE2's reworked socket system.
- **Skill Gem** — An item that grants an active ability when socketed into a weapon or gear skill slot.
- **Soul Core** — A PoE2 socketable item (similar to a Rune) that slots into weapons for a bonus effect.
- **Spirit** — A PoE2 resource, separate from Mana, that "reserves" capacity to sustain persistent effects — Auras, Minions, Heralds. Higher max Spirit lets a build run more or stronger persistent buffs at once.
- **SSF (Solo Self-Found)** — A self-imposed or league-enforced challenge mode: no trading with other players; everything must be found or crafted solo.
- **Standard** — The permanent, non-seasonal league that all characters eventually merge into once a temporary league ends.
- **Stance** — A combat mode certain weapons (e.g., Quarterstaff) or classes (Monk, Warrior) can switch between mid-fight, changing their available skills/moveset.
- **Support Gem** — An item socketed alongside a Skill Gem to modify its behavior (more damage, added effects, etc.), usually at a resource or cost tradeoff.

## T

- **TFT-equivalent** — PoE1 has "TFT," a large third-party Discord server used for bulk currency trading, paid crafting services, and carries. PoE2 has no comparably established third-party trading Discord as of 2026 — the built-in Currency Exchange and Instant-Buyout Merchant Tabs cover most of that need natively, in-client.
- **Trial of Chaos** — One of PoE2's two Ascendancy-point trial dungeons; an Ultimatum-style arena gauntlet.
- **Trial of the Sekhemas** — PoE2's other Ascendancy trial; a Sanctum-style roguelike relic run with in-trial permadeath stakes.
- **Twink / Twinked** — A low-level character deliberately geared with traded/stashed items to be far stronger than its level would normally allow.

## U

- **Uncut Gem (Uncut Skill Gem / Uncut Support Gem)** — PoE2's gem-acquisition system: a leveled, unspecialized gem item the player "cuts" (allocates) into a specific skill or support of their choice — replaces PoE1's random specific-gem drops.
- **Unique** — An orange-named item with fixed, predetermined (sometimes build-defining) special properties.

## V–W

- **Vaal Orb** — Currency that corrupts an item, permanently locking it out of further crafting while sometimes granting a powerful (or ruinous) random effect.
- **Waystone** — PoE2's endgame "map" item; used at a Waystone device to open an instanced endgame area, with tiers determining difficulty and reward.
- **Weapon Swap / Weapon Set** — A secondary equipped-weapon loadout a player can hotkey-switch to mid-combat.
- **Whisper** — A direct in-client message used to negotiate and execute a manual (non-Instant-Buyout) trade with another player.

## Z

- **Zoom / Zoomer Build** — A build optimized purely for movement and clear speed, at the expense of survivability or single-target damage.
