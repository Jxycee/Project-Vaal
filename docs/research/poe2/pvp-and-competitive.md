# PoE2 PvP and Competitive Play — Research

## Last updated
September 16, 2026

## TL;DR

Path of Exile 2 has **effectively no PvP** as of Early Access patch 0.5.5 "Forbidden Rites" (base patch 0.5.0 "Return of the Ancients"). There is no duel system, no PvP arena, no PvP ladder, and no seasonal PvP league — none of the several PvP modes PoE1 built up over a decade (1v1 duels, Sarn Arena free-for-all, 3v3 Capture the Flag, PvP-only seasonal ladders) exist in PoE2 today, and GGG has not committed to a PvP feature or date for the December 11, 2026 1.0 launch. Community feedback threads on the official forum explicitly flag this as a gap ("PoE 2 Without PvP Feels Like a Missed Opportunity," Dec 2025).

What PoE2 **does** have that's competitive in nature is entirely PvE-based:
- **Public per-league ladders** (character level, league-specific race categories) at pathofexile2.com/ladders — standard ARPG leaderboards, not player-vs-player content.
- **One-off GGG-run community race events** (fixed-seed, first-to-objective Solo Self-Found sprints) held periodically through EA, most notably a four-event **ExileCon 2026 qualifier series** (Aug 6/13/20/27) feeding a live on-stage finale at ExileCon in Auckland, November 2026.
- **Guilds**, which are a lightweight social/logistics wrapper (chat channel, shared identity, purchasable shared stash) with **no guild leveling, no guild achievements, and no guild-vs-guild content** of any kind.

No dedicated PoE2 esports scene or third-party tournament sponsorship (e.g., Twitch Rivals) has materialized as of September 2026; the closest precedent (Twitch Rivals "Path of Exile: Royale") is a PoE1-only event and has not been repeated for PoE2.

## 1. Does PoE2 have PvP?

**No dedicated PvP exists in Early Access as of 0.5.5.** Key evidence:

- No duel command/UI, no PvP arena zone, and no PvP-flagged league exists in the client as of the current patch. Multiple 2026 fan-wiki and guide sources (Game8, Fextralife) either state outright that dueling is not implemented or can only describe PoE1's PvP system when asked about PoE2's — i.e., there is no PoE2-specific PvP page/mechanic to document.
- Official forum thread "PoE 2 Without PvP Feels Like a Missed Opportunity" (Dec 2025) and an active "PvP in PoE2 and how to balance it" discussion thread confirm the community's read that PvP is simply absent, not just niche.
- PoE2 launched into Early Access in December 2024 with PvP unaddressed, and it has not been added in any of the numbered patches since (0.1 through 0.5.5).
- GGG has made no public commitment to a PvP mode, date, or design for the confirmed December 11, 2026 1.0 release. Recent Jonathan Rogers (game director) interviews around Gamescom 2026 covered class identity, itemization, endgame, and the 1.0 timeline — PvP was not a topic raised or answered in the interview coverage reviewed.
- Note a naming trap for future researchers: PoE2's 1.0 patch introduces a **"Duelist" character class** (melee stance-switching class). This is an ordinary PvE ascendancy-adjacent class name, unrelated to player-vs-player dueling — do not conflate the two when scanning patch notes.

**PoE1 precedent** (for comparison, not present in PoE2):
- PoE1 has run a mature PvP system since 2014: 1v1 **Duels** (challenge a party member, no loot on death), a free-for-all **Sarn Arena**, and **3v3 Capture the Flag** (Act 2 bandit-lord theme, flag capture/return scoring, GGG also experimented with 3-way 18-player CTF).
- PoE1 ran dedicated **PvP seasons** (e.g., Season 1: Dec 20, 2014 – Jan 18, 2015; Season 2: Feb 15 – Mar 16, 2015) with separate PvP ladders.
- PoE1 PvP has always been niche relative to PvE — GGG itself has described PvP investment as secondary to the core PvE/endgame loop, and that pattern has carried straight into PoE2's EA prioritization to date.

## 2. PvP game modes, balance, esports history

**Not applicable to PoE2 currently — there is nothing to describe.** No modes, no PvP-specific balance patches, and no PoE2 PvP tournament or esports history exists. This section is a placeholder pending any future GGG announcement; see Open Questions below for what to re-check as 1.0 approaches.

For historical context only: PoE1 PvP never developed a sustained competitive/esports scene beyond community and GGG-run seasonal ladders and the Capture the Flag events; it remained a side activity relative to PvE league racing, which is where GGG's actual competitive/spectator investment has gone (see Section 3).

## 3. Race events and leaderboards

This is where PoE2's real "competitive" surface area lives — all PvE.

### Per-league ladders (ongoing)
- Public ladders at **pathofexile2.com/ladders**, mirroring PoE1's ladder structure. Confirmed categories include per-league overall ladders (e.g., "Forbidden Rites," "HC Forbidden Rites," "SSF Forbidden Rites," "HC SSF Forbidden Rites") ranked by character level/experience, plus special one-off race-event ladders (e.g., "Act 3 Boss Kill Race 1/2").
- These are standard "who leveled/progressed fastest or furthest" boards — no PvP component, no delve-depth ladder confirmed specifically for PoE2 in the sources checked (PoE1 has a Delve ladder; whether PoE2 has replicated it is unconfirmed — see Open Questions).

### One-off GGG race events
- GGG has run periodic **fixed-seed race events** through 2026, open to all platforms (PC and console), typically Solo Self-Found on a fixed zone layout, first-to-objective format (e.g., a May 25 – June 8, 2026 three-event series; a "Last of the Druids" SSF race event with the Demigod's Virtue-style unique reward chain).
- **ExileCon 2026 qualifier series**: four qualifying races (Aug 6, 13, 20, 27, 2026), each a Solo Self-Found sprint to be first to kill **Tavakai in Act Four**, fixed layout, with a death penalty (character immobilized 2.5 minutes). Each qualifier's winner receives flights, accommodation, an Ultra VIP ExileCon ticket, an on-stage finale slot, and the unique **Demigod's Virtue** helmet. A player can only win once, spreading prizes across four different winners. The four qualifiers feed a **live finale on the ExileCon main stage** in Auckland, November 7–8, 2026 — this is the closest thing PoE2 has to a "competitive event" and it is a PvE speedrun format, not PvP.

### Community race site
- **poe-racing.com** is referenced as a long-standing community (not GGG-official) hub for organizing/tracking PoE race events; historically PoE1-focused, unclear how actively it's tracking PoE2-specific races (unconfirmed — see Open Questions).

## 4. Guilds

- Guilds exist in PoE2 EA as **a logistical/social wrapper only**: a guild chat channel, a shared guild identity/hideout customization, and a purchasable **guild stash**.
- **No guild experience, guild levels, or guild achievements** — direct characterization from research sources, i.e., guilds do not "progress" the way a character or Atlas does.
- **Guild stash**: guilds start with none; the guild leader purchases stash tabs with points (e.g., Premium Guild Stash Tab, Guild Map Stash Tab — 576 waystones per tier across 6 sub-stashes, Guild Socketable Stash Tab — up to 5000 of each socketable). The leader configures per-tab permissions by rank: fully open (anyone withdraws), contribution-only (members deposit, can't withdraw), and restricted (trusted ranks only).
- **Joining**: invite-only in EA — no in-client guild browser/search exists; players coordinate via chat or third-party sites to find an invite.
- **No guild-vs-guild content of any kind** — no guild wars, no guild leaderboards, no cooperative guild-only bosses or events found in any source checked. This is a firm gap, not a partial feature.

## 5. GGG esports/competitive investment specific to PoE2 (2026)

- **No dedicated PoE2 esports scene or sponsorship found.** Searches for tournament sponsorships, competitive circuits, or prize-pool esports events specific to PoE2 in 2025–2026 turned up nothing beyond the ExileCon race-qualifier series described above (which is a PvE speedrun format with travel/cosmetic prizes, not a structured esports league).
- **Twitch Rivals "Path of Exile: Royale"** (2021 team-of-two event, $30,000 prize pool; a 2022 rerun with Twitch Drops) is a **PoE1-only** creator event and has not been repeated for PoE2 in the sources checked.
- GGG's visible 2026 competitive-adjacent investment is concentrated entirely in the **ExileCon race-qualifier-to-finale pipeline**, timed to the game's pre-1.0 marketing push (ExileCon Nov 7–8, 2026; 1.0 launch Dec 11, 2026) — framed as a community/marketing showcase rather than an esports program.
