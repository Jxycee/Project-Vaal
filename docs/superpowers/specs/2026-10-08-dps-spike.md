# DPS spike (board item 27): can we reproduce PoB2 main-skill DPS?

Status: measured spike, nothing wired into the UI. Artifact: `docs/superpowers/oracle/dps-spike.json` (per build: PoB's own intermediates, our numbers, the exact factor decomposition, a crude second tier). The throwaway harness that wrote it was removed (it was ~190 lines, ran in 0.5 s, and its tier-2 tally is too crude to keep as an oracle); the method is below, rebuilding it is an hour.

## Verdict

**No class of build can show a DPS within 5% of PoB2 today. The best of nine comparable builds is at 13% of PoB's number with the simplest model, 35% with a crude second tier.** The miss is not a few missing mods: for 8 of 9 builds the per-hit damage alone is 7x to 4,000x short. Showing a number now would be wrong by an order of magnitude or more. Do not ship one.

## What was measured

Oracle: the `PlayerStat` block inside each fixture's own PoB code (decoded). For the main skill it carries `AverageDamage`/`AverageHit`, `Speed`, `CritChance`, `CritMultiplier`, `TotalDPS`. `TotalDPS` equals the fixture's poe.ninja `skills[].dps` row (checked), so "theirs" is exactly what poe.ninja shows, and PoB's intermediates let the error be split exactly instead of guessed.

Ours (tier 1, "ignore everything else"): `skills.json` per-level `baseMultiplier` x weapon hit (`bases.json` base + the weapon's own local lines: added phys/ele, % increased Physical, local attack speed, local crit, quality as 1% phys per point), x `attackSpeedMultiplier`; spells use the statSet's per-level min/max base damage and `castTime`/`critChance`. Crit at base chance, multiplier 2.0. The weapon set is the one the code had active (swap slots when `useSecondWeaponSet`).

Exact decomposition: `theirs/ours = hitDamage x critUplift x speed x perUseMultiplier`, reproduced to 1e-6 for every build (`decompositionExact`). `perUseMultiplier = TotalDPS / (AverageDamage x Speed)` is 1.00 to 1.05 (Twister, Ice Shot: extra hits per use).

Eligible: 9 of the 13 ordinary builds. Skipped, no hit DPS in the main skill: ci-acolyte (Ghost Dance), ci-disciple, ci-es-disciple (minion/command skills), hybrid-1 (Profane Ritual, DoT only).

## What dominated the error

1. **Per-hit damage (8 of 9 builds).** Everything that moves the hit: increased/more from all 19 gear slots and jewels, "gain X% of damage as extra Y", conversion, ascendancy and keystone effects, support more-multipliers with level scaling, flat added damage from gear and skills, runes. Our engine has none of the offence side: `collect.ts` reads defence stats only (damage stats are not collected).
2. **Crit (every build, 1.2x to 13x).** PoB crit is 24% to 100% with multipliers 2.75 to 15.2 (base 2.0): increased crit chance, added multiplier, "bifurcates", crit-conversion mods, all unmodelled. In caster-1 the crit uplift alone is 12.9x.
3. **Speed (1.2x to 2.7x).** Global attack/cast speed from gear, tree, supports (Rapid Attacks, Considered Casting is a *slow* cast), plus per-skill speed lines. Quiver/off-hand implicits are global, weapon lines local; the split is easy, the full sum is not.
4. **Not a factor:** weapon base, `baseMultiplier`, statSet base damage and cast time read cleanly from the synced data. hybrid-2's base hit lands within 15% with zero modifiers from the tree. The data side is ready.

## Failure modes found while building it

- Main skill selection: `mainSocketGroup` indexes ALL `<Skill>` elements and `mainActiveSkill` indexes *active* gems of the group (meta gems like Cast on Elemental Ailment count). The picked skill is confirmed by TotalDPS equalling a poe.ninja row for all 9; the row is named after the skill in 3 of 9 and after the wrapping meta gem or group in the rest (Ice Nova under Cast on Block, Comet under Cast on Critical), so the name check is weaker than the number check.
- v.N keys in statSet levels index the statSet's `stats` array, not the compacted list of varying stats.
- Gear "+N to Level of all ... Skills" is not counted (deadeye's bow gives +3 Projectile levels), so the gem is read 3 levels low there.
- Bonded rune lines skipped; whether PoB counts them was not checked.
- Fixtures carry no offence breakdowns (only defence), so per-modifier gap lists, which drove the defence work, do not exist for DPS. Only whole-factor errors can be measured.

## Honest remaining-work estimate

A faithful DPS engine is a second engine, the size of the defence one or larger:

1. Offence stat table + collector for damage/speed/crit/projectile/area stats across tree, gear (local vs global), jewels (radius), runes, ascendancy: the defence engine took ~10 rounds to get 13/13 on 13 builds with ~1 pool of stats; offence has ~5x the stat vocabulary and many more conditionals. **4 to 6 rounds.**
2. Skill pipeline: gem level (+gear levels), statSet selection (Impact/Tornado/Arrow/Shards...), support stat maps with level scaling (`statMap` calls exist in skills.json), conversion order, extra-as, damage types and per-type increased pools, projectile/hit counts per use. **3 to 4 rounds.**
3. Crit/speed/hit-chance/ailment (bleed, ignite) maths and PoB's rounding, enemy defences (armour, resists, configured boss). **2 to 3 rounds.**
4. Oracle to steer it: extend `fetch-oracle-builds` to keep the per-stat modifier breakdowns for offence, or the work is blind. **1 round.**

Total about 10 to 14 rounds like the defence ones before a first build reaches 5%, and attack builds with simple damage (bow/spear, one element) are the first plausible target; minion, DoT and trigger-meta builds (4 of 13 here have no direct hit at all) are out of reach for a long time.

## Recommendation

Do not show a DPS number. If a planner-visible offence figure is wanted sooner, the honest cheap options are factual ones that are exactly computable today: weapon base hit, attacks per second after local speed, skill base multiplier and level, labelled as "base, before modifiers". Revisit a real DPS number only after step 4 (offence oracle with breakdowns) exists, then run it one simple class (bow/spear single-element attack) first.
