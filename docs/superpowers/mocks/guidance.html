FAQ

3 questions
Can I run this without the unique bow?

Yes. Use a rare bow with the same two stats, you lose roughly a fifth of the damage but the rotation is unchanged.

Why Resolute Technique early?

It removes the crit roll from the early game and makes damage steady. It is dropped at the Pinnacle checkpoint.

What if I cannot reach resistance cap?

Swap the amulet for the resistance one in the Gear tab; it costs a little damage.
